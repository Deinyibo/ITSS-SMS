Iriebe Town
Secondary School

Iriebe Town Secondary School (UBE) is a government secondary school located in Iriebe Town, Port Harcourt, Rivers State, serving the community under the Universal Basic Education programme. The school is committed to providing quality, free and compulsory basic education to every child.

With dedicated teachers and a safe learning environment, the school prepares students in the junior secondary classes for success in senior secondary education and beyond, instilling the values of hard work, discipline and service to the community.
